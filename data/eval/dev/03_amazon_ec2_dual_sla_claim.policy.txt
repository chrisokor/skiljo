Amazon EC2 SLA — Dual Claim Structure

EC2 offers two distinct SLA credit paths: a region-level SLA and an
instance-level SLA. A customer may submit a claim under the region-level SLA
for a Region Unavailable event, and separately may be eligible for
automatic instance-level credits. Credits under the two paths do not offset
one another and may both apply to the same billing cycle.

To submit a region-level claim, you must include "SLA Credit Request" in the
subject line, the affected dates and times, and the affected resource IDs,
and submit it by the end of the second billing cycle after the incident
occurred. Claims that omit required information or miss the deadline are
denied.
