Twilio Terms of Service — Late Fee and Suspension

Payment is due within 15 days of the invoice date. Invoices unpaid after this
15-day grace period accrue a late fee of the lesser of 1.5% per month or the
maximum rate permitted by applicable law.

If an invoice remains unpaid for 45 days past the invoice date, Twilio may
suspend the account's ability to send messages or make calls until the
outstanding balance, including accrued late fees, is paid in full.
