Atlassian Refund Policy — Renewals and Upgrades Carve-Out

New Cloud subscription purchases are eligible for a refund within the
standard window (first paid month for monthly billing, 30 days from purchase
for annual billing). However, Cloud subscription renewals and upgrades to an
existing subscription are not eligible for a refund, regardless of how
recently the renewal or upgrade occurred.

This carve-out exists because renewals and upgrades extend or expand access
you already had, rather than constituting a new purchase decision.
