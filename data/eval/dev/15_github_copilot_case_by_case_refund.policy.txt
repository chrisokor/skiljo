GitHub Copilot — Operational Refund Flow

GitHub's Terms of Service state that there are no refunds or credits for
partial months of service, downgrade refunds, or refunds for unused months
with an open account, with no exceptions.

Separately, GitHub's published support flow for Copilot subscriptions
describes a 30-day refund window for an unused Copilot subscription, handled
case-by-case through the support Virtual Agent. Support agents evaluate each
request individually against usage data before approving or denying it.
