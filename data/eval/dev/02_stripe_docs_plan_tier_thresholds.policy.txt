Stripe Bundled Pricing — Plan Tier Thresholds

The Foundations, Expansion, and Scale plans differ in included payment
volume before overage pricing applies. Foundations includes up to $250,000 in
annual payment volume; Expansion includes up to $5,000,000; Scale includes up
to $50,000,000. Payment volume processed above your plan's included amount is
billed at your plan's marginal overage rate for the remainder of the annual
period.

If your account exceeds its plan's included volume, Stripe notifies you and
recommends upgrading to the next tier; no automatic upgrade occurs without
your consent.
