Vercel Pro Plan — Included Allocation Cascade

Your Pro plan includes a base allocation of 1TB of Fast Data Transfer and 10
million Edge Requests per month. Usage within the included allocation incurs
no additional charge.

Usage beyond the included allocation cascades to the monthly usage credit:
overage is first covered by your remaining $20 usage credit at standard
per-unit rates. Once the usage credit is exhausted, any further overage is
billed directly at the standard per-unit overage rate on your next invoice.
