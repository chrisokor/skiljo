Square Payment Terms — Reserve Withholding and Recovery Authorizations

If Square determines, in its discretion, that a merchant account presents an
elevated risk of chargebacks, refunds, or other liabilities, Square may
withhold a reserve from the merchant's settlement funds until the risk
period has passed.

Separately, if a merchant incurs fees, fines, or penalties imposed by a card
network or Square related to chargebacks or excessive refund activity, the
merchant authorizes Square to debit the merchant's linked bank account for
those amounts (a "Recovery Authorization") without further notice.
