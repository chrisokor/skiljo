AWS Audit Manager SLA — Baseline Credit Tier

If the Monthly Uptime Percentage for AWS Audit Manager is below 99.9% but at
or above 99.0% during any billing cycle, you are eligible for a Service
Credit of 10% of your charges for that billing cycle. If the Monthly Uptime
Percentage is below 99.0%, you are eligible for a Service Credit of 25%.

There is only one SLA tier structure for Audit Manager; unlike EC2 or Google
Compute Engine, there is no separate instance-level or configuration-based
credit path.
