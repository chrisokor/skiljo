Stripe Subscription — Taxes and Refund Exceptions

All Subscription fees are exclusive of applicable taxes, which are added to
each invoice based on your billing address. Subscription fees are
non-refundable and non-cancelable once paid, except as required by
applicable law.

Notwithstanding the foregoing, if Stripe discontinues a Subscription Plan
feature you were actively using and paying for, and no reasonably comparable
replacement is offered, you are entitled to a prorated refund of fees paid
for that feature for the remainder of the current billing period.
