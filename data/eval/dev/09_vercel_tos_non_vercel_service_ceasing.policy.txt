Some features available through Vercel are provided by third parties
("Non-Vercel Services"). If a Non-Vercel Service you have paid for through
Vercel ceases to be offered, Vercel will issue a prorated refund for the
unused portion of fees attributable to that Non-Vercel Service for the
current billing period.

This refund right applies only to Non-Vercel Services; it does not extend to
core Vercel platform features, which remain governed by the standard
all-fees-nonrefundable policy.
