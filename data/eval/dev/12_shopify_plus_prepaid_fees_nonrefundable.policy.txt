Shopify Plus Terms — Prepaid Fees

Plus Plan fees are invoiced in advance according to your Order Form and are
non-refundable and non-cancelable once paid, regardless of your actual usage
of the platform during the contract term.

If your contract is terminated by Shopify for your material breach, all
remaining prepaid fees for the unexpired term are forfeited and no refund is
due. If your contract is terminated by you for Shopify's uncured material
breach, Shopify will refund a prorated portion of prepaid fees for the
unexpired term.
