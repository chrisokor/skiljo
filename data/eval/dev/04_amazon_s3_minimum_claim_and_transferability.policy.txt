Amazon S3 SLA — Minimum Claim and Transferability

Service Credits are calculated as a percentage of the total charges for S3
usage in the affected billing cycle, and any Service Credit amount below
$1 USD will not be issued. Service Credits may not be transferred or applied
to any other account.

Service Credits are your sole and exclusive remedy for any unavailability or
non-performance of S3, and are mutually exclusive from any other remedy
available under your agreement with AWS, unless otherwise stated.
