Notion Refund Policy — Platform-Specific Purchase Routing

Where you purchased your subscription determines how you must request a
refund. Purchases made through the Apple App Store must be refunded directly
through Apple using Apple's own refund request process — Notion cannot issue
refunds for App Store purchases directly.

Purchases made online at notion.so or via Google Pay on Android should be
directed to Notion support at support@notion.so, and are handled under
Notion's standard refund windows.
