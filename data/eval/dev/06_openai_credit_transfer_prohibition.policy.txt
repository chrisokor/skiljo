OpenAI Service Credit Terms — Transfer Prohibition

Service Credits may not be sold, transferred, or gifted to any other account
or individual. Any attempt to transfer, sell, or gift Service Credits may
result in revocation of the credits without refund, and OpenAI may, at its
discretion, suspend or terminate the associated account.

Service Credits expire one year from the date of purchase and are
non-refundable at all times, including upon expiration.
