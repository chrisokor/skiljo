Vercel Anomalous Use

Vercel may, in its sole discretion, identify usage on your account as
elevated, irregular, high, or otherwise anomalous relative to your plan's
expected usage patterns. Vercel reserves the right to automatically charge
your payment method for fees accrued from anomalous use, or to suspend the
offending resource, at its sole discretion.

There is no fixed numeric threshold that defines "anomalous use" — Vercel
evaluates the totality of usage patterns, including sudden spikes, unusual
traffic sources, and deviation from the account's historical baseline.
