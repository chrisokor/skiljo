Amazon EC2 SLA — Automatic Instance-Level Credit

In addition to the region-level Service Level Agreement, EC2 provides an
automatic Service Credit for individual instance-level outages. If a single
instance is unavailable for more than six consecutive minutes (an
"Instance-Level Outage"), AWS automatically applies a Service Credit to your
account for the affected instance's usage during the outage window.

No support case or claim submission is required for the instance-level
credit — it is applied automatically based on internal monitoring data.
This is separate from and does not count against the region-level SLA credit
claim process, which still requires a claim submitted by the end of the
second billing cycle after the incident occurred.
