Stripe Subscription Plan Changes

When you change your Subscription Plan, the timing of the change and any
associated charge or credit depends on the direction of the change.

Upgrades: If you upgrade to a higher-priced Subscription Plan, the new plan
takes effect immediately. We will charge you a prorated amount for the
remainder of the current billing period based on the difference between your
old and new plan prices.

Downgrades: If you downgrade to a lower-priced Subscription Plan, the change
takes effect at the start of your next billing period. You will continue to
have access to your current plan's features and Entitlements until the
current billing period ends. We do not issue prorated refunds for downgrades.

Same-price changes (e.g., switching between plans of equal price): the change
takes effect immediately with no proration.
