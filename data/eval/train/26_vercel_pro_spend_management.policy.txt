Vercel Pro Spend Management

Your Pro plan includes a $20 monthly usage credit applied automatically to
on-demand overage charges. Once the included credit is exhausted, additional
usage is billed at standard per-unit overage rates.

Notifications: Vercel notifies the account owner by email once cumulative
usage reaches 75% of the monthly usage credit, and again when the credit is
fully consumed.

Spend management: By default, a Spend Management cap of $200/month is
applied to on-demand overage charges. Once cumulative overage charges reach
the configured cap, further resource usage that would incur additional
charges is paused until the next billing cycle unless the account owner
raises or removes the cap.
