Amazon EC2 Service Level Agreement

Region-Level SLA

AWS commits to making Amazon EC2 available with a Monthly Uptime Percentage of at least 99.99% for regions where you have deployed EC2 instances across multiple Availability Zones.

Region-Level Credit Tiers:
- Less than 99.99% but equal to or greater than 99.0%: 10% credit of monthly charges
- Less than 99.0% but equal to or greater than 95.0%: 30% credit of monthly charges
- Less than 95.0%: 100% credit of monthly charges

Instance-Level SLA

Individual EC2 instances receive an Instance-Level Uptime Percentage of at least 99.5%.

Instance-Level Credit Tiers:
- Less than 99.5% but equal to or greater than 99.0%: 10% credit of monthly charges
- Less than 99.0% but equal to or greater than 95.0%: 30% credit of monthly charges
- Less than 95.0%: 100% credit of monthly charges

Automatic Instance Credit

AWS will not charge you for any Single EC2 Instance that is Unavailable for more than six consecutive minutes within a clock-hour. This credit is applied automatically without requiring a claim submission.

Claim Requirements

To receive a Service Credit for SLA violations (other than the automatic instance credit), you must submit a claim by opening a case in the AWS Support Center. Your claim must:
- Include "Amazon Compute SLA Credit Request" in the subject line
- Specify the dates and times of the unavailability incident
- Identify the affected AWS region and Availability Zone
- List the affected EC2 resource IDs

Claims must be received by the end of the second billing cycle after which the incident occurred.

Exclusions

The SLA does not apply to unavailability caused by: factors outside AWS's reasonable control; customer actions or omissions; customer equipment or third-party software; or AWS's suspension of the customer's account under the AWS Service Terms.
