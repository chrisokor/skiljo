Stripe Bundled Pricing Plan Terms

Minimum contractual commitment

Bundled plans require a monthly commitment. Your subscription begins at 00:00 UTC on the day after you sign up, and runs for one month. After your plan starts, it can take up to 24 hours for all features to be available in your account. After you enroll, you are responsible for subscription fees and any applicable additional fees incurred for the full term, even if you schedule a cancellation before your contract ends.

Automatic subscription renewals

Subscription plans automatically renew for another one-month term unless you schedule a cancellation before the renewal date. If you do not want to automatically renew, you need to schedule a cancellation of your subscription before the auto-renew date.

Cancel a subscription

To prevent automatic renewal, you must:
- Email pricingbundles-support@stripe.com at least 2 business days in advance of your renewal date
- Have administrator access to the account

After your current contract period ends, your account automatically transitions to pay-as-you-go pricing for continued use of any products or features, and you can no longer access any unused entitlements or features that are only available through a subscription.

Subscription fees

Stripe subscription plans require a monthly commitment. When you subscribe, you agree to pay for the full monthly term, including any incurred additional fees. Payments are non-refundable, and we do not offer prorated refunds if you cancel before the end of your term.

Refund policy for cancellation of pre-paid subscriptions

We do not offer prorated refunds on pre-paid subscriptions. All Stripe subscription plans require a monthly commitment. You are responsible for the full subscription fee, including applicable overage fees for the entire term. We do not offer refunds (prorated or otherwise) if you cancel your subscription before the end of your current term.
