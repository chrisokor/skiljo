Stripe Subscription Suspension and Payment Failure

If a payment for your Subscription Plan fails, we will retry the charge
according to our standard retry schedule. If payment continues to fail for
more than 15 days after the original due date, we may suspend your access to
the Service until payment is received.

If your Subscription remains suspended for 30 consecutive days or more due to
non-payment, we may terminate your Subscription. Upon termination for
non-payment, any outstanding fees remain due and no refund is issued for the
terminated period.

Reinstatement: If you bring your account current within the 30-day suspension
window, access is restored immediately upon successful payment. No late fee
is assessed for the first payment failure in a rolling 12-month period; a 1.5%
monthly late fee applies to any subsequent failure in that window.
