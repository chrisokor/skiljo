Atlassian Refund Policy

Eligibility

Refunds are available only for initial purchases of Atlassian products. The following conditions apply:

Monthly Cloud Subscriptions

Refunds for monthly cloud subscriptions are only available within the first paid month after the trial period. After the first paid month, monthly subscriptions are non-refundable.

Annual Cloud Subscriptions

New annual cloud subscriptions can only be refunded within 30 days of the first purchase. After 30 days, annual subscriptions are non-refundable.

Data Center Licenses

Data Center licenses can only be refunded within 30 days of the first purchase.

Excluded Categories

Cloud renewals and upgrades cannot be refunded. This exclusion applies regardless of the time elapsed since the renewal or upgrade was processed.

Marketplace Apps

Marketplace apps follow the same 30-day refund window as annual subscriptions, though individual Marketplace vendors may consider exceptions outside this period at their discretion.

Enterprise Subscriptions

This refund policy does not apply to Enterprise subscriptions. Enterprise refund terms are governed by the applicable enterprise agreement.

How to Request a Refund

Contact Atlassian Support to initiate a refund request. You must provide your account information, the product and subscription details, and the reason for the refund request.
