OpenAI Services Agreement — Minimum Commitment and Late Fees

Enterprise customers with a minimum monthly commitment must pay the greater
of (a) actual usage fees or (b) the committed minimum, for each month of the
Term. Committed minimums are not refundable and cannot be applied to a future
term.

Invoices are due within 30 days of receipt (Net 30). Any amount not paid
within 30 days of the due date is subject to a late fee of 1.5% per month, or
the maximum rate permitted by applicable law, whichever is lower, accruing
from the due date until paid in full.

If a customer disputes a portion of an invoice in good faith, the customer
must pay the undisputed portion by the due date; the disputed portion is not
subject to a late fee while the dispute is pending resolution.
