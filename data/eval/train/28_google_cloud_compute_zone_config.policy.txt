Google Cloud Compute Engine SLA — Instance Configuration

The monthly uptime percentage credit ladder depends on how your instances are
configured.

Instances in Multiple Zones: If your Covered Instances are configured to run
in multiple zones in more than one region, and the monthly uptime percentage
falls below 99.99% but at or above 99.0%, you receive a 10% credit. Below
99.0% but at or above 95.0%, a 25% credit. Below 95.0%, a 50% credit.

Single Zone or Single Instance: If your Covered Instances are configured to
run as a single instance or within a single zone, the monthly uptime
percentage must fall below 99.5% but at or above 99.0% for a 10% credit,
below 99.0% but at or above 95.0% for a 25% credit, and below 95.0% for a 50%
credit.

In no event will total credits for any billing month exceed 50% of the
amount payable for that month, regardless of configuration.
