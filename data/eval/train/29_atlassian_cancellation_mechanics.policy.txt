Atlassian Cancellation Mechanics

Paid subscriptions: If you cancel a paid Cloud subscription, your
subscription remains active through the end of the current billing cycle. A
15-day grace period after the billing cycle ends is provided during which you
can reactivate without losing configuration or data; the subscription is
deactivated at the end of the grace period if not reactivated.

Free tier: If you cancel a free-tier product, deactivation is immediate upon
cancellation — there is no grace period because no billing cycle applies.
