GitHub Marketplace Billing

If you downgrade or cancel a Marketplace app's paid plan, no refund or credit
is issued for the unused portion of the current billing cycle. Your current
plan, and any features or usage limits associated with it, remains active
until the end of the billing cycle in which you downgraded or canceled; the
new (lower or free) plan takes effect at the start of the next billing cycle.

Upgrades to a higher Marketplace plan take effect immediately, and the
prorated difference for the remainder of the current cycle is charged at the
time of the upgrade.
