Cloudflare SLA — Tier-Gated Eligibility

SLA eligibility depends on your plan tier. Free and Pro plan accounts are not
covered by any uptime SLA and are not eligible for Service Credits under any
circumstances. Business plan accounts are covered by the 100% uptime
guarantee described in the Business SLA. Enterprise plan accounts are
covered by a separate Enterprise SLA that applies credit multipliers of 10x
or 25x depending on which support success-tier the customer has purchased.

A customer's plan tier at the time of the outage determines which SLA
(if any) applies — retroactive upgrades after an outage do not confer
eligibility for that outage.
