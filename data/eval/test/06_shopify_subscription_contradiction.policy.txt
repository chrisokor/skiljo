Shopify — Terms of Service vs. Help Center (Refund Contradiction)

Shopify's Terms of Service state plainly: "You acknowledge and agree that
Shopify has no obligation to provide you with a refund of any prepaid fees
for any reason, and that no refunds will be given under any circumstances."

However, Shopify's published Help Center article on the refund policy for
subscriptions states that customers can contact Shopify Support to request a
refund, and that "eligibility is reviewed case-by-case" based on time since
the charge and reason for the request, with typical approval for requests
made within a short window of the billing date and a clearly stated reason
(such as accidental renewal or plan change confusion).

These two statements describe different practices for the same decision: the
ToS asserts an absolute no-refund position while the Help Center describes an
operational review-and-approve process that grants refunds under stated
conditions.
