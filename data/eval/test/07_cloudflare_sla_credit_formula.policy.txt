Cloudflare Business SLA — Credit Formula

For qualifying Business plan accounts, if actual uptime falls below the
100% uptime guarantee in a calendar month, Cloudflare calculates a Service
Credit using the formula:

Service Credit = (Outage Period in minutes x Affected Customer Ratio) /
Scheduled Availability in minutes

The resulting Service Credit is expressed as a percentage of the fees paid
for the affected service during the affected month, and is applied as a
credit toward a future invoice. Cumulative Service Credits in any rolling
12-month period are capped at one month's cumulative fees.
