Steam Refunds — Downloadable Content (DLC)

DLC purchases are refundable within 14 days of purchase, provided the
playtime of the underlying base game has not increased by more than 2 hours
since the DLC was purchased, and provided the DLC itself has not been
consumed, modified, or transferred (for example, a redeemable in-game item
that has already been redeemed).

If the DLC has been consumed, modified, or transferred, it is not eligible
for a refund regardless of days since purchase or playtime.
