Cloudflare Business SLA — Exclusions

The following categories of downtime are excluded from Service Credit
calculations and do not count as an "Outage Period" under the SLA:

(a) scheduled maintenance announced at least 24 hours in advance,
(b) factors outside Cloudflare's reasonable control (force majeure),
(c) the customer's own equipment, software, or network,
(d) the customer's breach of the Terms of Service,
(e) suspension or termination of the customer's account for cause, and
(f) DNS issues not directly caused by Cloudflare's network.

If a reported outage falls into any of these six categories, it is excluded
from the Outage Period calculation entirely, even if Cloudflare's systems
were otherwise degraded during the same window.
