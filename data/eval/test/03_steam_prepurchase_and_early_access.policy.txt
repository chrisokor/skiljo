Steam Refunds — Pre-Purchases and Early Access

Pre-purchased titles: the standard 14-day refund window does not begin until
the game's official release date, regardless of how far in advance the
pre-purchase was made. Playtime accrued before release still counts toward
the 2-hour playtime limit.

Early Access titles: because Early Access games are playable before the
"official release," playtime accrued during Early Access counts toward the
2-hour limit from the moment of purchase — there is no release-date deferral
for Early Access titles the way there is for standard pre-purchases.
