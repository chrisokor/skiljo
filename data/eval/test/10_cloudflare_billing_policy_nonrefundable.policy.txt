Cloudflare Billing Policy — Fees Are Nonrefundable

FEES ARE NONREFUNDABLE. All fees paid for Cloudflare services are
non-refundable except as expressly stated otherwise in your agreement with
Cloudflare or as required by applicable law. There is a minimum one-month
purchase obligation for all paid plans; canceling mid-month does not entitle
you to a refund or credit for the remainder of that month.

This billing policy is independent of the separate SLA Service Credit
process: an SLA Service Credit is a credit toward a future invoice for
uptime shortfalls, not a refund of fees already paid, and does not override
the fees-are-nonrefundable billing policy.
