Google Cloud Storage SLA — Sibling of Compute Engine

Google Cloud Storage offers a Monthly Uptime Percentage SLA structurally
similar to Compute Engine but with different credit tiers: below 99.95% but
at or above 99.0% yields a 10% credit; below 99.0% but at or above 95.0%
yields a 25% credit; below 95.0% yields a 50% credit.

Unlike Compute Engine, Cloud Storage has no instance-configuration
distinction (no multi-zone vs. single-zone branching) — a single credit
ladder applies uniformly regardless of storage class or bucket
configuration. The claim notification window is 30 days, matching Compute
Engine's own claim window.
