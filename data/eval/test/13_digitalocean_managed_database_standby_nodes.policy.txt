DigitalOcean SLA — Managed Databases (Standby Nodes)

Managed Database clusters configured with Standby Nodes carry a 99.95%
monthly uptime SLA. Managed Database clusters without Standby Nodes carry a
lower 99.5% monthly uptime SLA, reflecting the reduced redundancy of a
single-node configuration.

Credit tiers otherwise follow the standard structure: below the applicable
threshold but at or above 99.0% yields a 10% credit; below 99.0% but at or
above 95.0% yields a 25% credit; below 95.0% yields a 100% credit. Claims
must be submitted within two billing cycles of the incident.
