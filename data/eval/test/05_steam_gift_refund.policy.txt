Steam Refunds — Gifts

Unredeemed gifts: a gift that has not yet been redeemed by the recipient can
be refunded by the purchaser within 14 days of purchase, following the same
playtime-based rules as a normal purchase (since an unredeemed gift has zero
playtime by definition, this generally means unredeemed gifts are refundable
within the 14-day window).

Redeemed gifts: once a recipient has redeemed a gift, only the recipient can
request a refund, and the request is evaluated against the recipient's own
playtime and the original purchase date — not the redemption date. Steam
also monitors accounts for a pattern of frequent refund requests, which may
result in additional review or restriction of future refund privileges.
