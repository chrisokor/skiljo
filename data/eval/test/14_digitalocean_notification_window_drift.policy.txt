DigitalOcean SLA Family — Notification Window Drift

Across DigitalOcean's per-product SLAs, the claim notification window varies
by product: Droplets, Volumes, Databases, and Spaces allow 30 days; App
Platform, NAT Gateway, and Container Registry allow only 24 hours;
Regional Load Balancers require notification within "two billing cycles" of
the incident (a framing distinct from a fixed day count).

This drift is not explained by any stated policy rationale in the public SLA
pages — the products share the same underlying credit-tier structure and
differ only in notification window length and framing, which is consistent
with independent per-product drafting rather than a deliberate tiering
decision.
