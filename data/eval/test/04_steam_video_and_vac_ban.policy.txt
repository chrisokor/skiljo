Steam Refunds — Video Content and VAC Bans

Video content (movies, series) purchased individually is non-refundable
once any portion has been viewed, and non-refundable after 14 days
regardless of viewing status. Video content purchased as part of a bundle
follows the bundle refund rules instead.

VAC bans: if a VAC (Valve Anti-Cheat) ban is issued against an account for a
game, the customer forfeits all refund rights for that game, regardless of
days since purchase or playtime. This forfeiture applies even if a refund
request was already pending at the time of the ban.
