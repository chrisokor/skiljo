Steam Refunds — Bundles

A bundle purchase is eligible for a full refund only if none of the
individual items within the bundle have been transferred (for example,
gifted or traded), and the combined playtime across all items in the bundle
is less than 2 hours, and the request is made within 14 days of purchase.

If any single item in the bundle has been transferred, or if combined
playtime across the bundle reaches 2 hours or more, the entire bundle becomes
ineligible for a refund — partial refunds for a subset of bundle items are
not offered.
