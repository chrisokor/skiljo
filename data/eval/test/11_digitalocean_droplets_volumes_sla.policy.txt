DigitalOcean SLA — Droplets and Volumes

Droplets and Volumes are each covered by a 99.99% monthly uptime SLA. If
monthly uptime for the affected resource falls below 99.99% but at or above
99.0%, you receive a 10% Service Credit. Below 99.0% but at or above 95.0%,
a 25% Service Credit. Below 95.0%, a 100% Service Credit.

To claim a credit, you must notify DigitalOcean support within 30 days of
the incident, including the affected resource ID and the incident window.
