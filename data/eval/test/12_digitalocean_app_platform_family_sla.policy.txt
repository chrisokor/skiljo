DigitalOcean SLA — App Platform, NAT Gateway, and Container Registry

App Platform, NAT Gateway, and Container Registry each carry a 99.95%
monthly uptime SLA — a lower guarantee than Droplets and Volumes (99.99%).
Credit tiers follow the same percentage-based structure: below 99.95% but at
or above 99.0% yields a 10% credit; below 99.0% but at or above 95.0% yields
a 25% credit; below 95.0% yields a 100% credit.

Claims for these three products must be submitted within 24 hours of the
incident's resolution — a materially shorter window than the 30-day window
for Droplets and Volumes.
