Shopify Terms of Service — Payment, Fees, and Termination Sections

Source: https://www.shopify.com/legal/terms
Retrieved: 2026-08-10
Excerpt scope: Section 5 (Payment of Fees and Taxes) and Section 9.3 (POS
Services), limited to refund, credit, and billing-on-termination language.
Quoted verbatim from the published Terms of Service; section numbers refer
to Shopify's own numbering at the time of retrieval and may shift when
Shopify revises the document.

Section 5 — Payment of Fees and Taxes

No Refunds Policy

"Shopify does not provide refunds."

This is stated as a general, unconditional position and is not qualified
anywhere in Section 5 by an exception process, a review window, or a
customer-support escalation path.

Subscription Billing

"Subscription Fees are paid in advance and will be billed in 30 day
intervals."

Because Fees are paid in advance of the period they cover, a mid-cycle
cancellation does not by itself create an overpayment to be returned; the
Terms treat the advance payment as fully earned once billed.

Non-Refundable Upon Termination

"Unless otherwise provided in the Terms of Service, you will not be
entitled to any refunds of any Fees, pro rata or otherwise."

This clause explicitly forecloses pro-rata refunds on early termination,
whether the termination is initiated by the merchant or by Shopify.

Outstanding Balance Due

"Any outstanding balance owed to Shopify for your use of the Services
through the effective date of such termination will immediately become
due and payable in full."

Termination accelerates any unpaid balance rather than pausing or waiving
it — the obligation to pay survives account closure.

Final Invoice

"If there are any outstanding Fees owed by you at the date of termination
of the Service, you will receive one final invoice via email."

The final invoice is administrative (it documents what is owed) and is not
described as a mechanism for requesting a refund or credit.

Section 9.3 — POS Services

No Refunds for Certain Equipment

"Returns and refunds are not available for the Chip & Swipe Reader, Swipe
(Audio Jack) and physical gift cards."

This extends the no-refund position from subscription Fees to specific
categories of hardware and stored-value instruments, with no case-by-case
exception language attached.

Summary of the written position

Read together, Section 5 and Section 9.3 state an absolute, unqualified
no-refund policy for both subscription Fees and the enumerated hardware
categories. No time window, support-escalation path, or "case-by-case"
review process appears anywhere in these sections of the Terms of Service.
This is the document to contrast against Shopify's own Help Center refund
article (see shopify-help-center.txt in this corpus), which describes a
support-reviewed, time-windowed exception process that Section 5 does not
mention. That gap is the corpus's flagship intra-vendor
policy-vs-practice contradiction (see docs/POLICY_CORPUS.md, entry #12).
