Section 5 — Payment of Fees and Taxes

No Refunds Policy

"Shopify does not provide refunds."

This is stated as a general, unconditional position and is not qualified
anywhere in Section 5 by an exception process, a review window, or a
customer-support escalation path.

This is the document to contrast against Shopify's own Help Center refund
article (see shopify-help-center.txt in this corpus), which describes a
support-reviewed, case-by-case exception process that this clause does not
mention. That gap is the corpus's flagship intra-vendor policy-vs-practice
contradiction (see docs/POLICY_CORPUS.md, entry #12).
