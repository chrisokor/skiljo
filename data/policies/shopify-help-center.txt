Shopify Help Center — Refund Policy for Subscriptions

Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/your-account/manage-billing/refund-policy-subscriptions
Retrieved: 2026-08-10
Excerpt scope: the full refund-eligibility guidance for Shopify plan
subscriptions, quoted verbatim from the published help article.

Core Policy Statement

"Shopify plans are generally non-refundable as stated in section 5.10 of
the Shopify Terms of Service."

Note the internal cross-reference: this article points back to the Terms
of Service as the governing document, but the numbering it cites (5.10)
does not match the Section 5 language captured in shopify-tos.txt in this
corpus, which is itself a minor drift signal worth flagging independently
of the main contradiction below.

Case-by-Case Review Path

"If you believe you've been charged in error or have a special situation,
then you can contact Shopify Support to have your case reviewed."

Unlike the Terms of Service, which states no exception process at all,
this article establishes a live escalation path: a merchant can contact
Support and have a human reviewer consider the request.

Eligibility Time Windows

The article specifies concrete consideration periods, in the merchant's
favor, during which a refund request will be looked at:

"Within 7 days of invoice issue date" — for monthly subscriptions.

"Within 30 days of invoice issue date" — for annual subscriptions and for
the first invoice issued after a trial period ends.

"Within 14 days of invoice issue date" — for payments made via SEPA Direct
Debit.

These are affirmative windows during which Shopify Support will consider a
request — they do not appear anywhere in the Terms of Service excerpt.

Important Caveat on the Windows

"Meeting these time windows doesn't guarantee a refund. Refund requests
are reviewed on a case-by-case basis by Shopify Support."

This caveat keeps the final decision discretionary, but it does not negate
the existence of the review process itself — a process the Terms of
Service does not describe or reference.

What Is Not Eligible

The article states that the following are "generally not eligible" for
refund consideration:

"Plan downgrades."

"Requests outside of time windows."

"Unused services" — because "not using your Shopify store during your
billing period doesn't automatically qualify you for a refund."

Theme Purchases

"Theme purchases from the Shopify Theme Store are final sale and
non-refundable."

This item is consistent with the Terms of Service's absolute no-refund
stance and is not part of the contradiction — it is included here because
it is the one line in this article that squarely matches the ToS position
rather than diverging from it.

Why This Pairs With shopify-tos.txt

The Terms of Service (shopify-tos.txt) states, without qualification, that
"Shopify does not provide refunds" and that merchants "will not be
entitled to any refunds of any Fees, pro rata or otherwise." This help
article instead documents a support-mediated, time-windowed, case-by-case
exception process for exactly the same subscription Fees. The two
documents describe materially different customer experiences for the same
decision surface (can a merchant get a subscription fee refunded), which
is the acceptance case named in docs/POLICY_CORPUS.md entry #12 and in
CLAUDE.md's description of commit A3 (cross-document contradiction
detection).
