Section 6 — Payments and Billing

6.1 — Payment Obligations

"You agree to pay us, through the Payment Processors or as otherwise
agreed to by DigitalOcean, all sums for Services you select or use at
applicable prices."

6.3 — Non-Refundable Commitments

"Your purchase of committed usage, reserved capacity, or a similar
consumption commitment (e.g., Required Monthly Usage or a Term Spend
Minimum) is non-cancelable and non-reducible, and the unpaid remainder of
such commitment(s) is immediately due."

This clause treats committed-usage purchases as locked in for their full
committed value; canceling early does not reduce what is owed, and any
remaining unpaid portion accelerates immediately.

6.3 — Account Deactivation for Nonpayment

"We reserve the right to deactivate, terminate, prevent access to,
disable services for, and/or delete any Accounts or access to the
Websites and Services at any time at our sole discretion, including for
nonpayment, late payment, or failure to charge your Payment Methods."

Nonpayment is named explicitly as a deactivation trigger, and the
resulting action can extend as far as account deletion, not just a
temporary service pause.

Section 8.5 — Beta Services Termination

"Your access to and use of each Beta Service will automatically terminate
upon the release of a generally available version of the applicable Beta
Service or upon notice of termination by us."

Beta access is time-bounded by design — it ends either at GA release or at
DigitalOcean's discretion, whichever comes first.

Section 12 — Termination

General Termination Policy

"We reserve the right, in our sole discretion, to terminate your access to
all or any part of the Websites and/or Services at any time, with or
without notice."

Non-Refundable Fees

"Except as otherwise set forth herein or expressly agreed between the
parties, any and all fees paid to us are non-refundable and any and all
fees still owed to us at the time of such termination shall be immediately
due and payable."

Summary of the written position

Across Sections 6 and 12, DigitalOcean states an unconditional
non-refund position for fees already paid, combined with acceleration of
any fees still owed at termination — mirroring the "outstanding balance
due immediately" language in the Shopify Terms of Service excerpt in this
corpus (shopify-tos.txt). The one qualifier present is "except as
otherwise set forth herein or expressly agreed between the parties," which
is a hook for negotiated enterprise exceptions but is not itself a
described process, unlike Shopify's Help Center case-by-case path. This
file is included in the corpus as a second no-refund-plus-acceleration
baseline, useful for testing whether the cross-document detector correctly
treats structurally similar clauses from different vendors as independent
findings rather than merging them.
