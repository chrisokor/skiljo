Stripe Subscription Policy — Fees, Overages, and Cancellation Refunds

Source: https://stripe.com/legal/subscription-policy
Retrieved: 2026-08-10
Note: the task brief's suggested source (stripe.com/guides/payments-
regulations-by-country) is a jurisdiction guide, not a refund policy
document. This excerpt instead uses Stripe's own Subscription Policy,
which is the document named in docs/POLICY_CORPUS.md entry #1 as the
prototype customer policy for Skiljo — it combines a flat subscription fee
with metered overage charges and states an explicit non-refund position.
Quoted verbatim from the published policy.

Section 2 — Minimum Commitment

"Each Subscription Plan requires a monthly or annual commitment. After you
enroll, you are responsible for Subscription Service Fees and any
additional Fees incurred for the full term, even if you schedule a
cancellation before your Subscription Plan ends."

The commitment survives a scheduled cancellation: fees keep accruing for
the full term regardless of when the cancellation is scheduled.

Section 4 — Cancel a Subscription

"Either you or Stripe may cancel your Subscription Plan at any time. Any
cancellation will take effect at the end of your then-current Subscription
Plan term."

Cancellation is always deferred to term-end; there is no described path to
an immediate cancellation with a partial-period refund.

Section 6 — Subscription Fees and Overage Fees

"A Subscription Service Fee covers usage up to the entitlement limit for
your Subscription Plan during your current term...If usage exceeds your
entitlement, overage Fees will apply for this excess usage."

Entitlements are usage caps bundled into the flat fee; usage beyond the cap
is billed separately as an overage, which is a distinct fee category from
the base Subscription Service Fee.

Section 8 — Charge Schedule

"You will be charged your recurring Subscription Service Fee at the start
of your Subscription Plan and again at the start of every subsequent
billing period...You will be charged for any additional Fees, including
overage Fees, at the end of the billing period that they were incurred or
another cadence as communicated to you."

Base fees are billed in advance; overage fees are billed in arrears, after
the usage that generated them has already occurred.

Section 12 — Refund Policy for Cancellation of Subscriptions

"Fees are non-refundable, and we do not offer prorated refunds if you
cancel before the end of your Subscription Plan term. You are responsible
for the full Subscription Service Fees, including applicable Taxes and
overage Fees for the entire Subscription Plan term."

This is the controlling refund clause: cancellation before term-end does
not create any refund obligation, prorated or otherwise, and it does not
relieve the customer of overage Fees already incurred during the term.

Summary of the written position

Stripe's Subscription Policy states an explicit, unconditional
non-refund position for both the flat Subscription Service Fee and any
overage Fees, tied to a term that only ends at the originally scheduled
renewal date regardless of when cancellation is requested. Unlike the
Shopify pair in this corpus, no companion help-center document is included
here describing a discretionary exception process — this file is included
in the corpus primarily as a clean, single-document non-refund baseline
for comparison against the Shopify (no-refund ToS vs. case-by-case help
article) and Cloudflare (no-refund clause vs. discretionary credit
language) pairs.
